Rules

Routing threshold
A complaint goes to a department when the model is at least 0.45 sure, else Needs routing.
Deadlines
Payment: 15 business days, 35 when extended. Others: 8 weeks.
Unusual rise
At least 2.0 times the usual weekly cases (last 4 weeks) and at least 10 cases.
Urgent
Deadline past or within 5 business days, or the notes mention vulnerability, money hardship or ongoing fraud. A person can also set it, with a reason.
