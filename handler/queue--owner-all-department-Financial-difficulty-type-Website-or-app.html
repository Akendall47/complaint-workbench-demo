How this page works

Pick the next case to work on. The case closest to its deadline comes first.

  1. Read down the list. Cases past their deadline are at the top.
  2. Use Filters to narrow the list, or the tabs to see unassigned cases.
  3. Select Assign to me on a case with no owner.
  4. Select a case number to open the case.

One table sorted by deadline, so the most urgent work is always the first row.
